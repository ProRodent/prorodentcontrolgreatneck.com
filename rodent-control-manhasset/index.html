Reading the house before the traps go down.

Dryer vents, foundation vents and gaps around hose bibs are small, easy to overlook and common entry points regardless of the age of the house. A vent flap that does not close fully or a missing screen is worth mentioning when you call.

Properties with more surrounding green space tend to report outdoor burrow signs first, while tightly spaced interior blocks tend to report indoor sounds and droppings first instead.
